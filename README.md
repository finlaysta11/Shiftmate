# ShiftMate

Student jobs that fit around your classes.

ShiftMate is a clickable prototype for a Business Research Methods 1 (BRM1) research project at Hanze University of Applied Sciences. It connects job shifts with the university timetable, so students only see shifts in their free time.

**Open the app:** https://finlaysta11.github.io/Shiftmate/

On your phone, open the link and choose **Add to Home Screen** to install it like an app.

## What you can try
- Connect a university timetable (demo, no real login)
- See only shifts that fit your free time, including urgent shifts with a higher hourly rate
- Accept a shift in one tap
- Swap a shift with another student
- Rate employers on fair treatment and paying on time
- Track hours and earnings, with a weekly hour limit
- Pause shifts during exam days

All employers, shifts and ratings are demo data.
